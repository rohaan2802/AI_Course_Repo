Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AL2002) at FAST-NUCES Islamabad. The repository has the lecture slides, three Python assignments (multi-robot path planning, a genetic algorithm and an expectiminimax game), the final project brief, quiz and exam solutions, the lab notebooks from two batches, the textbook and my study notes.

Unit 1

Agents and search

Intelligent agents and environments, uninformed search (BFS, DFS, UCS, IDDFS) and informed search (greedy, A*).

Unit 2

Optimisation and constraints

Local search and hill climbing, genetic algorithms, constraint satisfaction and cryptarithmetic.

Unit 3

Games

Adversarial search, minimax, alpha-beta pruning and expectiminimax with chance nodes.

Unit 4

Machine learning

Regression and gradient descent, KNN, decision trees, K-means clustering, perceptrons and neural networks.
